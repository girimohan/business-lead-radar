# Legitimate interest assessment (LIA): business prospecting

GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) needs a written balancing test before relying on legitimate interest. Review it once a year,
or when the radar starts collecting something new. Date and sign at the bottom.

## 1. Purpose test: is there a legitimate interest?

- **Interest:** offering Barmo's services (websites, AI receptionist, bookkeeping) to small local businesses
  whose public information suggests the service fits them. Recital 47 GDPR: direct marketing may be regarded as
  a legitimate interest.
- **Benefit to the recipient:** a relevant, small-business offer from a local provider, with a reason given.

## 2. Necessity test: is the processing needed, and is there a less intrusive way?

- Only business information is used: name, business ID, industry, address, website, and contact details the
  business itself published on its own website. No home addresses, no personal social-media profiles (only the business's own Instagram/Facebook link if its site shows one), no data bought
  from brokers, no profiling beyond "which of my services fits this business".
- Sole traders are not emailed without consent: the first contact is a call, letter or visit, where they
  can say no at once.
- One message at a time, written for that business, never a mass mailing.

## 3. Balancing test: do the individual's interests override it?

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Reasonable expectation | Businesses publish these details so customers and suppliers can reach them; B2B offers are common. Sole traders are a closer call, so they get the stricter channel rule. |
| Nature of the data | Business contact data only; no special categories. |
| Impact | One message or call; a "no" ends it permanently. |
| Safeguards | Art. 14 notice in the first message; opt-out list checked on every run; data kept on one computer (git-ignored) and, for the daily online run, only encrypted with a passphrase GitHub does not have; uncontacted leads deleted after 180 days; website cache pruned; a contact log for accountability. |
| Vulnerable groups | None targeted. |

**Conclusion:** the interest is legitimate, the processing is limited to what is needed, and with the
safeguards above it does not override the interests of the businesses or their owners.
